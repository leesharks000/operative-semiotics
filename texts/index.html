Texts

Full texts of the discipline's constitutive works, published flat: one work or notebook per file, plain markdown, no pagination, no scripts, no login. The complete Grundrisse runs to 1.3 MB as a single file, which is unreadable for most retrieval and composition systems; here it is served as nine notebooks plus front matter, each independently fetchable and each carrying its own provenance header.

Provenance. All works are by Johannes Sigil (Lee Sharks), and every file's canonical record is a deposit of the Crimson Hexagonal Archive, content-addressed by AXN identifier. Operative Semiotics is the study and design of signs that intervene in the systems in which they circulate; it is not Charls Pearson's Theory of Operational Semiotics, nor the Organizational Semiotics of the Stamper school, nor Greimasian operativity — see the disambiguation papers. Notebook X: The Gate (open, accreting)

workshop edition · plates, orderings, and open seams · read the notebook · canonical record #1550 · manifest.json

The tenth notebook works the admission boundary between retrieval and composition. It is a workshop rather than a treatise and is designed to stay one: material accretes as plates, structure is a view over them, and collisions between perspectives are declared as seams and held open rather than synthesized.
