**The seam is a new operator class — suspension-mediated, not repair-mediated.** Where a repair-mediated operator produces its effect *through* the receiver's normalization, a seam produces its effect by **preventing the normalization from completing**. The effect is **epistemic holding**: the reader must maintain two incompatible positions without resolution. A reader who merges it anyway — or a system that cannot process open contradiction and discards the document.**
