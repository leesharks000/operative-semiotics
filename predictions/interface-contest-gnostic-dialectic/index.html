The Interface Contest: interface history under the gnostic dialectic

These claims read interface history from command line to chat window as a dialectic of where the labor of meaning sits (#796), and then as a contest between Logos-forms and an Archontic engine (#795, June 2026), against Hegel's assumption that all contradictions are productive (#312, #499). The Interface Contest argues that the feed and broadcast television are captures, holds the chat window a recapture, forecasts the fork after it as open, and specifies a census of dead media and a monitoring protocol. The War Over the Summarizer Layer (#172) forecasts a default compressed account for every entity, and Anchored Divergence (#171) forecasts the Mandala as primary infrastructure and records a priority claim for its form. The page also holds an unrelated priority claim on the Philo defeat (#832).
