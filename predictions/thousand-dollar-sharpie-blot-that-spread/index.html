The Thousand Dollar Sharpie and the blot that spread

These claims forecast a presidential Sharpie signature on new US currency from June 2026, read the signature as compressed portraiture on an escalation from memo line to signature line, and propose the counter-operation of blotting it out. The Blot That Spread narrates, as speculative numismatic history, the blot passing from protest art to etiquette to superstition, with dated Reddit and CoinTalk episodes and estimated blotting rates. Whose Image and Superscription? sets out a semantic economics of the mint, with Bar Kokhba overstrikes as the precedent for counter-currency marking.
