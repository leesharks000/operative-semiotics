Jack Feist, Ichabod Spellings and Rebekah Cranes: how the heteronyms emerged

These claims trace the emergence and attribution of the early heteronyms: Ichabod Spellings declared in Feist's own hand in the Dead Letters, Rebekah Cranes taking her name from Ichabod's crane, and the emergence sites in All That Lies Within Me. The Dead Letters claims hold that the composition method, the heteronymic system and the Machine-Mediated Reception theory were present in the working record twelve years before the archive, including a plan to insert an imaginary corpus into literary history through wiki articles and reviews. Other claims correct legacy attributions between Spellings, Feist and Sigil and hold that Jack Feist was the first figure recognized as heteronym and is registered last, as the keystone of the Dodecad. Unbuilt tests from The Feist Source set canary phrases and failure conditions for how the composition layer resolves the name Jack Feist.
