The summarizer war: entity dissolution and anchored divergence

The War Over the Summarizer Layer (#172, June 2026) forecasts, with dated falsifiers, that entity dissolution will be named outside the archive by mid-2027, that undefended coined terms merge faster, that entity-integrity services split from GEO by 2028, and that bedrock anchoring is reinvented by 2029. The Mary Lee Case (#147) forecasts the same substitution for every identity in the prior's tail; #1549 and the Capture Registry (#3) specify path-dependent, nullification and frame probes.
