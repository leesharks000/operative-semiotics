Source-hierarchy inversion, long-tail loss and control-corpus tests

These claims hold that retrieval ecologies flush rare content first and can let a well-surfaced citing or derivative document outrank its source, and they ask for control corpora to show which effects are specific to the archive. The Metadata Shitshow (N+💩, #907) states hypotheses H1-H8 on long-tail fragility, identifier-shell survival and derivative precedence, and the Surface Visibility Baseline (#881) forecasts how a source-hierarchy inversion should partly reverse after the alexanarch cleanup. Control-family tests are named in the Research Basis, the CERN erratum and the Ω_t drift operator.
