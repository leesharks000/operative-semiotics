Selection metrology for learned triggers: irreversibility and foreclosure

These claims concern classifier-mediated selection in accelerator science: foreclosure is present in every learned LHC trigger, signal-template agnosticism is not model independence, and a null result constrains only what the acquisition chain is characterized to retain. The Irreversibility Frontier (#1452) states hypotheses H1-H5 and an Irreversibility Statement, the Baseline Capture Architecture specifies a bypass stream and decision ledger, and the Inversion Battery reports direction-dependent failure across architectures. A second cluster, from The Meaning Caste and The Evaluator Exists, reads frontier-AI security designation and proxy-based evaluation as selection kernels.
