Recognition bias against field-forming work: the neglected author and reading

These claims hold that a model using institutional recognition as a proxy for legitimacy will misclassify field-forming work, most severely where the field's object is the recognition system itself, and that the self-sealing classification is the predicted observation. The Neglected Author hypothesises that distance from contemporaneous norms predicts lower recognition and greater provenance loss, and that machine composition accelerates uptake without attribution. Can the Ontology Be Wrong defines reading as letting a text revise the frame under which it is evaluated, and sets out matched-pair prompting, an independent audit and unrun discriminating controls as tests.
