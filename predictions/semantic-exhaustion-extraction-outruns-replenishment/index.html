Semantic exhaustion: extraction of meaning outrunning its replenishment

The Semantic Exhaustion summary of January 2026 (#264) holds that semantic exhaustion is underway: extraction of meaning exceeds replenishment across human, institutional and model substrates, and model collapse is the same crisis in another substrate. #264 specifies measures such as the Semantic Debt Ratio, Exhaustion Proximity Index and Regeneration Capacity Assessment; The Anthropological Limit (#133, May 2026) states that its instruments do not measure exhaustion as a whole. The Semantic Economy framework (#18) forecasts platform capture of interpretive frameworks and claims that burnout tracks semantic rent. The Pergamon Reclamation (#32) argues that Pergamon Press industrialized the enclosure of scholarly meaning, the Sorting Function (#746) that non-predatory mediation was outcompeted at scale, and the Groundskeeper's Treatise (#533) holds commons-depletion thresholds. The page also holds unrelated claims on coverage architecture, operative metadata, Guillory and other subjects.
