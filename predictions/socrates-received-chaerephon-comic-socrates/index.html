Socrates received: Chaerephon, the comic Socrates and the authorship notebook

Historical theses that the Socrates of the corpus answers a prior, comic Socrates, that comedy supplied the position's notoriety and the later corpus its operation, and that Chaerephon's biographical record descends from the scholia to Clouds 144. The Chaerephon Problem reads him as a recurring authorial function, and The First Draft sorts him as a datum for the received-character operation rather than a name in the regress. Notebook rounds of the authorship investigation add the maker who must divide, the person as what answers in account, and the corpus that survived with its disputes intact.
