Money as a transmission channel: what the money-form cannot carry

September 2026 semantic-economy papers hold that what money cannot carry loses power over the account (#1618), restated by #1620 as a requirement that it acquire a monetary or model proxy; that a monetary scalar cannot natively preserve a prohibition on its own equivalence (#1622); and that the native monetary form preserves only properties invariant under substitution (#1625). #1625 forecasts that the 2026 signature inscription will transmit as a security mark and not as a name. Monetary Dark Matter (#1631) holds regime conditions with falsifiers: obligating provenance lowering value in some extraction regimes, and universal inscription turning a flight to quality within a currency into a flight out of it. The page also holds an unrelated 2014 memoir line.
