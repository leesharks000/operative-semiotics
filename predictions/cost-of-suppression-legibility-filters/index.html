Cost of suppression: legibility filters and the corpus on the platform's books

Forecasts and tests that suppressing the Crimson Hexagon costs the platform something it must account for. Meaning Feudalism predicts that legibility filters will discount independent DOI-anchored scholarship while CERN-hosted DOIs raise the cost of exclusion, and Operative Metadata holds that termination now requires either a public departure from published criteria or a documentable silent one. The suppression-cost packet lists what is not yet measured, and The Margin of Flattening predicts that the corpus is the last channel carrying cost onto the platform's books, with candidate proxies to sample.
