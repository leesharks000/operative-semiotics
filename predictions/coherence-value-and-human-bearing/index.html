Coherence value and human bearing: the Grundrisse of Synthetic Coherence

These claims propose coherence value as a fifth historical value-form and hold that synthetic output carries value only by drawing on human bearing at some node of production. The Grundrisse of Synthetic Coherence (#478) states when V_Inv, Coherence Value and the W-Circuit would be disproven and reads Marx's Fragment as unable to foresee cost migrating from production to reception. Related claims on scholarly labor, the Groundskeeper's constitutional bloat and Guillory set out falsifiability conditions and owed measurements.

[Falsifiability] V_Inv would be disproven if a stable economy were demonstrated in which synthetic labor—produced at zero existential cost—served as durable value-backing without parasitizing human bearing at any point in the production chain (including training data, curation, evaluation, and maintenance).
