Adversarial by Origin: security taxonomies as law without courts

Adversarial by Origin (#194, June 2026) forecasts that AI security taxonomies will formalize origin-based adversariality as de facto law by checklist, with no court deciding the question. Its forecasts cover the pleading that will be copied, the EU AI Act as a source of importable definitions and the defense choosing the first litigated case, and it states falsifiers for a twenty-four-month window. Its historical thesis traces the asymmetry to Tim Wu's Machine Speech, and the godkinggoogle.com map (#1384) summarizes the argument.
