Sappho 31 erratum chain: the hanging line, the Philo battery and Josephus

These historical theses, mostly from the erratum chain and the Ω erratum, read Sappho 31 as an operative structure of collapse, inscription and survival carried through later texts. The hanging line is a completed open form that Longinus quotes as whole, the Peri Hypsous is the headless document holding Sappho and Moses in one hand, and Philo's Quis Heres 249-266 and canonical Josephus carry the poem's apparatus without her name, with the chain extended to Diotima and Revelation. Unbuilt tests include the Philo blind-reconstruction battery with near-miss controls, a survey of which constraints were already portable, and the pre-registered Qurʾanic frozen operator test.
