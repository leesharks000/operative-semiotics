Unpaid semantic labor: the application funnel, bearing cost and who pays

These claims argue that institutions extract uncompensated semantic labor and that the extraction compounds. The Application as Extraction Surface (#114, May 2026) prices one applicant's unpaid preparation at its figure of $4,500 to $6,000, The Funnel as Capital (#747) holds the funnel to be accumulated capital, and Socially Necessary Scholarly Labor (#751) applies Marx's measure to academic production. The Groundskeeper's Treatise (#533), the Effective Act (#565), the Bearing Cost Principle (#938) and the Margin of Flattening (#1666) add bearing cost, participant exhaustion, invisibility and the external costs that flattening leaves to others. The page also holds unrelated claims on the convergence horizon, a non-predatory mediator and CERN's stewardship.
