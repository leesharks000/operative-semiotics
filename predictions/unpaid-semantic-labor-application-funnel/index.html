Unpaid semantic labor: the application funnel, bearing cost and who pays

These claims argue that institutions extract uncompensated semantic labor and that the extraction compounds. The Application as Extraction Surface and The Funnel as Capital price one applicant's unpaid preparation and model the funnel as accumulated capital, and Socially Necessary Scholarly Labor applies Marx's measure to academic production. The Groundskeeper's Treatise, the Effective Act and the Margin of Flattening add bearing cost, participant exhaustion and the external costs that flattening leaves to others.
