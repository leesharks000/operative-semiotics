Governance without accountability: OKF, Moltbook and the Zenodo–CERN audit

These claims diagnose platform and repository governance that acts without accountability. The Conveyor Belt and the Compression Machine forecasts OKF's stages from adoption without output governance to a forced correction around 2028 and 2029, and the Moltbook diagnostics describe routing without governance among agents.
