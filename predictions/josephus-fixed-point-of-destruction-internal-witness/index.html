Josephus and the fixed point of destruction: the internal witness

The Fixed Point of Destruction states the internal witness condition: when a terminal destruction is known from a participant's testimony, the record is conditioned by the survival that made the testimony possible. A corpus rebuilt from survivors reproduces the selection, so Josephus at Jotapata and Sappho through Longinus are read from the position spared. The availability audit forecasts that the paper's term-space risks absorption into the combinatorics of the Josephus function, and it sets the T17 external-availability battery to re-measure its baseline.

PRED-1412.G1standing claimregistered 2026-07-28 in #1412 · The Fixed Point of Destruction: Josephus, the Counting-Out, and the Witness Destruction Must Leave If It Is to
Call this the internal witness condition: when a terminal destructive process is known through the testimony of one of its participants, the record is structurally conditioned by the survival that made the testimony possible. The condition does not make the witness truthful, and does not make the witness false. It makes the witness interested in the strict sense: the account exists only because its author occupies the outcome it describes. That is a relation among selection, survival, authorship, and evidentiary interest, and it is not a tautology — it is a constraint on what kind of evidence an internal account can be.
