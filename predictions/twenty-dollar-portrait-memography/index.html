These claims argue that the Bureau of Engraving and Printing's redesigns of the $20 portrait between 1996 and 2003 activated a latent resemblance that later became politically charged, while the aesthetic rationale went undocumented (#502, #1151, February 2026); #502 itself specifies the control faces and inter-rater study its comparison lacks. The Inauguration of Memography (#506) states four falsifiable predictions about evidentiary kernels in conspiracy memes, curatorial gaps, conventions and containment frames. All the Spoils of Babylon (#507), The Unclean Bill (#535) and the Grundrisse planning documents (#950) extend the dossier with curatorial prompting, a fatwa request and a pending FOIA request.
