Plato–Aristotle authorship: measures, controls and pre-registered predictions

The Measurement Register, the frozen pre-registrations and the authorship notebook set out what the Plato–Aristotle investigation measured and predicted: Aristotle completing executions Plato abandoned, his charges against Plato naming properties of his own corpus, the copy and assay vocabulary, and Aristotle as compiler of the record. Forecasts and unbuilt tests require labelled controls (the Attic orators, Albert→Aquinas, Sappho→Catullus) before the target is scored, and the ghost catalogues are read as one filing operation seen from two sides. The page states the strong claim, that Plato is Aristotle's heteronym, beside the ledger's graded standings, which hold one historical hand as live and not established.
