Many and One: one maker, the vacant centre and Theophrastus at the joint

These claims argue that the Plato-Aristotle corpus is the work of one person divided by voice, object and name, that what made him look like many authors is that he wrote about many things, and that Socrates not writing is the form the vacant centre takes. They read the κερματισμός metaphor across four loci, the just/unjust argument relabelled as weaker/stronger, and Theophrastus's On First Principles as the question to which Metaphysics Λ is the attempt, down to the memory question at De anima III.5. The Measurement Register and the Notebook also record the instruments' limits, such as the finding that lexical measures track period, not pupillage. Works: Many and One, EA-NOTEBOOK-01, EA-CORPORA-05 and -14, The First Draft.
