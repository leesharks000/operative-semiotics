The page reads ancient legitimacy as standing rather than paternity and sets the esoteric/exoteric partition as an Alexander story told at the seam where corpus meets empire. What Syllogizing Can Divide names the production model as one self-dividing maker, dividing the corpus where De anima III.5 divides mind, and leaves open tests on the ghost catalogues and on epigraphic items not yet held.
