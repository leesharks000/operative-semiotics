Effective acts: Ginsberg's declaration and the unauthorized performative

These claims define the effective act as an unauthorized declaration, with Ginsberg's declaration of the end of the war as the model: the Effective Acts summary (#413) argues it was meant literally, and the Protocol of Effective Acts v2 (#153, June 2026) that it worked by recursion rather than geopolitically. #153 holds a falsifier, sustained absence of recursion, and argues a contemporary case in the Baal effigy burned in Iran on 11 February 2026, whose semiotic prelude #528 argues dropped out of the war's coverage. #413 addresses a reader after the conditions have changed.
