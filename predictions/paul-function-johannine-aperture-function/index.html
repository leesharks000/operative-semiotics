Function, not biography: the Paul function and the Johannine aperture function

Historical theses from the Revelation First work plans (#1400, #832, June 2026) and staging document (#847) argue that named scriptural figures are author-functions rather than biographical individuals: Paul as the adversarial probe a heteronymic mystery system generates against its own indeterminacy, and the Baptist, Evangelist, Elder and Revelator as positions within one Johannine aperture-function. The Feist Source (#852) argues the same of the Logos, resolved by its archive into the biography of Jesus, and the Capture Registry (#3) reads an AI Overview card as carrying the Paul function at its sharpest. The page also holds unrelated claims on the strawberry diagnostic and on operative feminism.
