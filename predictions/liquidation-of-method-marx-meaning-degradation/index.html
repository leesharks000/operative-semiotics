The liquidation of method: the sign 'Marx' from Engels to Piketty

The Liquidation of Method (#501, February 2026) argues that Marx offered a method and that its reception, from Engels through Mao, Fanon and Piketty, turned it into content, with English 'for/against' forcing it into positions while the 1857 Introduction goes untaught. It specifies a liquidation audit and a method-versus-content contrast test, and forecasts the same capture for the Semantic Economy, Autonomous Semantic Warfare and the Crimson Hexagon. From Atomism to the Semantic Condition (#488) argues the 1841 dissertation is the metaphysical core of the system, and the Autumn Notebook (#991) argues Marx's work is a retrocausal loop. The page also holds unrelated claims on memography, the retrocausal turn, Ghost Code and the training-layer sequence.
