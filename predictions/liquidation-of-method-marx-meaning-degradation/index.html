The liquidation of method: the sign 'Marx' and the degradation of meaning

The Liquidation of Method argues that Marx offered a method and that its reception, from Engels through Piketty, turned it into content, with English 'for/against' forcing it into positions; it proposes a liquidation audit and a method-versus-content contrast test. The Prepositional Alienation and the Autumn Notebook forecast the same loss compounding through AI: intent defaults reproduced autoregressively in training data, a substitution spiral, attention collapse and a cost to sentience. The Infinite Tunnel adds that crawlable AI Mode share links put summaries into the corpus.
