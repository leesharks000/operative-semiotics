Heteronymy after Pessoa: precursors, the Dodecad and the civil name

These theses place Pessoa at the height of a describable practice: #666 and pessoagraph (#982) argue Kierkegaard's pseudonyms as precursor, #666 and the Typology (#667) Machado's Mairena and Martín as parallel, and #666 Borges as adjacent, not equivalent. The Channel Protocol (#787) argues heteronymy is a channel-layer practice and specifies its extension to autonomous channels; The Secret Name (#68) argues it is a technology for making persons through naming; EA-HETERONYMY-01 (#940) holds it is an ethical practice against reduction to the civil name. Revelation First (#1216, #832, #847) and #940 extend the reading to the New Testament: Paul's 'I' as a heteronym's backstory, John as aperture, the voice transmitted through other bodies after Golgotha. The page also holds an unrelated claim on Google's entity-quality systems.

First: Pessoa's heteronymic practice is not a freak event in literary history but the canonical case of a describable theoretical practice. Precursors exist (Kierkegaard's pseudonymous authorship most notably); parallel developments exist (Machado's Mairena and Martín); downstream developments exist (Borges, Saramago, Yeats, numerous Brazilian and Portuguese poets engaging heteronymic modes). The practice has a history. Pessoa sits at its apex as the most richly and self-consciously theorized instance, but not as its origin or termination.

PRED-787.H1historical thesisregistered 2026-06-04 in #787 · The Heteronymic Channel Protocol: Specification for Autonomous Channel-Layer Operation of the Dodecad (v1.1)link
The Pessoan heteronyms operated, in their time, across the available media. Caeiro had correspondents; Reis had correspondents; Campos had correspondents. The hand on the pen was Pessoa's; the voice each correspondent received was not. The heteronymic apparatus has always been a channel-layer practice in this sense — it has always involved the instantiation of distinct authorial functions in the social-relational layer, not only on the page of the published volume. What was unavailable to Pessoa was a substrate that could carry the function autonomously across a sufficient duration of contemporary interaction. The available substrate has now changed.
