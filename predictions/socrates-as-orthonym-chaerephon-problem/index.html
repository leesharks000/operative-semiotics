Socrates as orthonym and the Chaerephon problem

These claims read the Socratic-Platonic-Aristotelian corpus as one heteronymic configuration, with Socrates as the orthonym defined by the refusal to write and the Socratic problem reclassified as a symptom of that configuration. The Chaerephon Problem argues that institutional scholarship cannot tell multi-author transmission from mature heteronymy, that the method has no internal halting criterion, and that repeated philological failure is how the configuration is passed on. Further claims record the reading composed whole by Google AI Mode and AI Overview, its Kierkegaardian extension, and Socrates as a constructed figure in the Arch-Philosopher mantle object.

PRED-136.H4historical thesisregistered 2026-05-28 in #136 · The Chaerephon Problem: Heteronymy as Symbolon — Authorial Law, the Unbounded Configuration, and Propagation b
The mechanism is a cascade. Each generation of scholars meets the founding corpus, deploys the biographical instruments, fails at the same seam, names the failure (the Socratic problem, the Homeric question, the Synoptic problem, the authorship strata of the Pauline corpus), declares it unresolvable, and routes around it — and in routing around it, transmits the configuration intact to the next generation, who will deploy the same instruments and fail identically. The philological failure does not degrade the transmission. The philological failure is the transmission.
falsifier
Under instrument-defeat, the gaps are *functionally load-bearing* — they fall precisely at the seams where biographical and functional identity would have to be separated, the functional complementarity is *too well-fitted* (each position doing exactly the transmission-work the configuration requires), and every proposed separation *re-collapses* under pressure rather than resolving. Under mere scarcity, the gaps are *random* with respect to function — more evidence would resolve them, and the missing pieces are not the load-bearing ones.
