Marx's Grundrisse as form: the prosodic algorithm and general intellect

These historical theses read Marx's Grundrisse as a prose form whose conceptual modes produce distinct prosodic pressures, with a bimodal paragraph distribution, derivation sentences that end in formulas, and recorded self-corrections. The Four-Term Prosodic Algorithm and the Prosodic Asymmetry Algorithm turn these into application rules, and the HESPERUS plan draws the Muses as operators from Cha's Dictée. The Marx Room, the Grundrisse notebooks and The Voice of the Dead extend the reading to the value-form as operator derivation and to general intellect.
