Theophrastus as first draft: the seams with Λ, the Ethics and De anima

The First Draft and Aristotelian or Theophrastan read Theophrastus's Metaphysics fragment as the continuation of Λ, divided from it by name at the hinge where the account passes from complete to needing more. Forecasts state what each seam should show: Λ answering the fragment's aporiai, the Ethics' types containing the Characters' sketches plus the frame, De anima subordinating the De sensibus survey, and HP sharing HA's connective repertoire in a less varied form. The theses read the Theophrastan function as one operation at two scales and hold that the you of the Elenchi is the I of the fragment, so the corpus contains its own reception.
