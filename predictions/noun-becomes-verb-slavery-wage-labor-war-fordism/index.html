The Noun Becomes a Verb: slavery, wage labor and the Nazi labor economy

The Noun Becomes a Verb holds that slavery priced the person, the noun, while wage labor contracted capacity for an interval, the verb, and that the Civil War settlement put the verb under contract and debt while the unit of measurement shrank from life to day to task to motion. The Colony Enters the Factory holds that Lebensraum was turned in practice into a labor supply, that the conquered population was carried into Reich industry as a flow of persons, and that War Fordism kept the specified verb and made racial classification the switch that chose its coercive regime. Smaller residents from The Murder of the Vow forecast inverting the contract presumption from signed-equals-valid to invalid-unless-live-legitimacy, and Operative Feminism names women the citational record erased from its origins.
