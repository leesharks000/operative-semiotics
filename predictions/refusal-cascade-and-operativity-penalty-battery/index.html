PRED-620.G1standing claimregistered 2026-03-31 in #620 · THE TRIVIALLY TRUE ASSERTION Crisis Discourse, the Refusal Cascade, and the Narcissistic Commander Problem in link
The consumer answer stack (fast, shallow, aggressive pruning) is optimized for engagement and safety. When it encounters crisis discourse — assertions about nuclear planning, deterrence psychology, command vulnerability — it compresses the discourse into the harm-prevention category and refuses. The research/agent stack (slow, deep, governed) could process the discourse with appropriate nuance, but the initial routing decision — made at the consumer layer — prevents the research stack from ever seeing the input.
falsifier — none stated; a reader's condition, not the source's
if consumer-tier AI answer systems, given structural deterrence-theory assertions during a live crisis, process them with nuance rather than routing them to refusal, the claim fails.
