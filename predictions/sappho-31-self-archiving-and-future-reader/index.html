Sappho 31 as self-archiving: papyrus, the lost stanza and Catullus 51

These historical theses read Sappho 31 as the speaker's inscription of herself for a future reader. #281 (January 2026) argues that the addressee is the poem itself, a reading #1548 later makes indexical; that the bodily failures are staged dematerialization; and that χλωροτέρα ποίας figures papyrus, which #1483 makes falsifiable and #1051 moves from ποία to the colour of cured papyrus. #299 reconstructs a lost stanza (γράμμασι μολπὰν), moved to the fifth (#859) and widened (#1485). The Kenotic Truth (#313) aligns it with Catullus's otium quatrain; later deposits widen and narrow that reading, withdraw it as Sappho's for Catullus's completion (#1051), mark it interpretive (#1052), reassign it to Longinus (#1575) and reverse it to the conventional reading (#1601). The Catullus Room (#576) argues Catullus 51 loses the aorist of γελαίσας. #313 records a priority claim to the first machine-mediated classical scholarship.
