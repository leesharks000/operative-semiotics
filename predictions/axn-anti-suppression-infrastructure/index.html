AXN as anti-suppression infrastructure: claims, falsifiers and precedents

AXN as Anti-Suppression Infrastructure (#1094, July 2026; v5, #1606, September) states its properties as claims each with a falsifier: derivation integrity, record binding, ledger continuity, core integrity under amendment, custody, reconstructibility and the retrieval-layer hypotheses, and records custody and reconstructibility as design rather than demonstrated property; v5 holds its agreeing cross-runtime paths to be conformance evidence only. Its historical theses argue the same property in samizdat, the abolitionist press, NOWa, PGP and LOCKSS, and that every act of suppression leaves records that become evidence. The Symbolon specification (#1432) adds a deterministic regrowth gate, and #1543 reads the Masorah as a pre-digital integrity apparatus. The page also holds unrelated claims on memography, small presses, Afroman, prefix substitution and other subjects.
