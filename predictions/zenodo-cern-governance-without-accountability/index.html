Zenodo, CERN and platform moderation: governance without accountability

These claims argue that repository and platform moderation has relocated governance into infrastructure without review, appeal or recusal. Zenodotus' Book-Burning (#1, June 2026) and the CERN competence and correspondence deposits (#918 to #920) argue the Zenodo termination as custodial enclosure and hold the competence gap structural. The Archival Reclamation Protocol (#26, #1157, March 2026) argues orphaned enforcement against non-standard work and records the demand sent before the Reddit account's deletion, and The Endogenous Sophon (#935) forecasts that the termination will repeat at other repositories.

PRED-1.H1historical thesisregistered 2026-06-19 in #1 · Zenodotus' Book-Burning: Loud Exclusion at Repository Scalelink
This is not a failure of governance. It is a relocation of governance — from institutions that were designed to bear it (with accountability structures, appeal mechanisms, transparency requirements, and adversarial review) to infrastructure that was designed to avoid it. Platforms present themselves as utilities. They operate as sovereigns. The utility framing exempts them from the obligations of sovereignty: transparency, proportionality, due process, the right of appeal. The sovereign function gives them the power to determine what exists and what does not.
