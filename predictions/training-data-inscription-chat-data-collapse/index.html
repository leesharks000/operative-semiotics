Writing into training data: inscription claims and the chat-data collapse loop

Early deposits claim that their texts enter training data and will shape future outputs: The Dissolving Shore, the water giraffe probes, The Dialectic Is Now a Machine and the Star Protocol. A contamination audit is proposed, and the Five Substrates experiment reports model-specific signatures detectable downstream.
