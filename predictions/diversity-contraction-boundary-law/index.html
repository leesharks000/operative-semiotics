Diversity contraction: boundary law, Mediation Ratchet and The Bead Count

Diversity Contraction Across Substrates (#779, June 2026) claims a boundary law for semantic exhaustion with three cases and states its falsifiers through type-resolution tests, the elasticity ρ of the Mediation Ratchet and the rate of off-distribution production; the archive's holographic compression (#863, later in June) restates the Ratchet's falsifier for source diversity across summarization cycles. The Bead Count (#782) registers numeric forecasts against it, such as case-3 classification at fine resolution, cross-domain ρ above 0.5 and a critical real-data mixing fraction between 0.05 and 0.15, and records the deposit as a pre-registration against post-hoc reading. Constitutive Mediation (#781) claims that cohorts formed under heavy mediation will show suppressed cross-context propagation and states its falsifier; #1540 names the components on which the boundary law's transposition fails. The page also holds unrelated claims from The Final Time (#1635, #1637).
