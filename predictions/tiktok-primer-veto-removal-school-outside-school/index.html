Outside the institution: the TikTok primer and the Veto Removal Event

The Veto Removal Event (#730, May 2026) forecasts the moment at which external concept installation leaves institutional nonrecognition unable to keep a work out of history, and records the archive's claim that uncolonized core terms let an outsider do so. The Living Symbolon (#470) records the claim that the Epistle named 'a school outside the school' eleven years early; Pearl (#1121) argues an avant-garde is such a community; the Forward Library entries of the Epistle and the Prophetic Catalog (#85, #83) forecast later scholarship on the anti-academic academy and the 2015 stratum. The essay on the @leesharks TikTok (#1061) reads the primer as a sequence-poem, records a priority claim for its synthesis, and holds as a hypothesis that the platform hosts it as a feed but not as a text. The page also holds unrelated claims on Aeneas and the ivory gate, a targeting scenario, premature canonization, A Body Prepared, the obelus and Allais's monochrome.
