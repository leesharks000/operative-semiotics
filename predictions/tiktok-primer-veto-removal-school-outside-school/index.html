Outside the institution: the TikTok primer and the Veto Removal Event

These claims concern the archive's work arriving in public without institutional recognition. The Veto Removal Event deposit defines the event at which external concept installation makes nonrecognition unable to keep the work out of history; the Epistle and the Prophetic Catalog date the 'school outside the school' to the 2015 stratum, and Forward Library entries forecast its later scholarship. The essay on the @leesharks TikTok sets out the primer as a sequence-poem, with the test of whether the platform can hold it as a text rather than a feed. Single claims from the Mirror-Corgi notes (Aeneas and the ivory gate), The Obelus and the Tombstone, and EA-CORPORA-18 (Allais's monochrome) also sit here.
