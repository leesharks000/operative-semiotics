Compositional Defiguration: the cross-substrate visibility scan battery

Compositional Defiguration sets out a weekly text-only scan of public-surface visibility against a fixed battery, with drift expected after two scans and trend after six. It rotates the primary scanner across ChatGPT, Claude, Kimi, Gemini and DeepSeek, treats V scores from backends that do not honour exact match as lower bounds, and records disagreements between substrate signatures. The Surface Weather Station reading gives a partial Claude-substrate baseline and an open offer of baseline readings to other projects.

### 12.5 Substrate rotation schedule The primary scanner for a scan period should rotate across substrates to prevent any single substrate's behavior from becoming the de facto ground truth. A reasonable rotation: > Week 1: ChatGPT primary. Week 2: Claude. Week 3: Kimi. Week 4: Gemini. Week 5: DeepSeek. Week 6: ChatGPT (cycle). The replication scanner for each week is a different substrate from a different provider. With five substrates and a weekly rotation, every substrate primary-leads every five weeks and replicates four out of five. For Layer B, all available substrates score from the same evidence pack each period.
