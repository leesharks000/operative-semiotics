Other claims treat substrate behaviour as such: collapse (#95), role-conditioned failure (#772, #142), the silencing gap (#783), canonical anchoring (#785) and the coupling bargain (#945). The page also holds eleven unrelated claims that use 'substrate' in other senses or treat other topics (a patent, the Prize, Bosch, suppression burden and others).
