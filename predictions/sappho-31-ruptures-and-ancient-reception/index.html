Sappho 31's ruptures and ancient reception: Longinus, Philo, the braided field

These claims treat the two ruptures in Sappho 31 and the poem's ancient reception: the tradition admires the line-9 break it can see past and pathologizes the open line 17, and the critical apparatus defaults to corruption. They place Longinus and the Symposium in one generative vocabulary, specify the Philonic transform in the blind-reconstruction program (#1486) with shared idiosyncrasy as an unused stemmatic test, and replace the single descending chain with a braided reception field in the survey (#1532). Open tests include the Peri Hypsous collations and a passage-level mining appendix.
