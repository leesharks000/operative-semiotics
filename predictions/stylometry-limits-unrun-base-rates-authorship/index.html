Stylometry's limits and unrun base rates in the authorship investigation

Standing claims from the authorship investigation set limits on what stylometry can show: genre separates more strongly than authorship, separability is not identity (Chatterton as Rowley separates from Chatterton as himself), and no measured property recovers master-student succession from heteronymic construction. Unbuilt tests list the base rates and runs still owed, among them residual-vs-total parity, comparanda for terminal aporia, the Pessoa leave-one-out folds, work-level and register-conditioned runs on Aristotle and Theophrastus. Frozen predictions order speaker tightness by constraint severity and distinguish engineered from natural ambiguity, beside readings of the Statesman's open genus and Plato's coin figure.
