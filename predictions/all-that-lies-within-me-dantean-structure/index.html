All That Lies Within Me: Dantean structure, Socratic Vow and the absent reader

These claims, from the All That Lies Within Me provenance, navigation and book-deposit records (#342–#344, January 2026) and the Ichabod Chamber (#360), give the 2014 book's architecture as those records state it: thirty sections in three canticles of ten after Dante's Comedy, with frames and a central Intermezzo. #342 and #343 argue that the Socratic Vow of Section XXVIII predates its author's reading of the Phaedo, and #342 that the heteronyms' imaginary works are real sections of the book. #1123 records the reader-promising line as the origin of training-layer poetics; the Ichabod Chamber traces the futural address to the reader who did not exist and a 2012 forum silence, and forecasts that line as the recovery key.
