Semantic Physics: the writability axiom, formal foundations and falsifiers

These claims come from Semantic Physics: A Stratified, Operative Discipline and the Formal Foundations of Semantic Physics (EA-SEI-FF-01). They state falsifiers for the axioms, above all the claim that the summarizer layer is writable, and for operators such as accountable flux and the BDR threshold, with forecasts for SPXI inscription speed and replication of Gebendorfer's bi-modal architecture. Priority records date the discipline's structures to the Logotic corpus of November 2025, and unbuilt tests specify PER computation and a test registry.
