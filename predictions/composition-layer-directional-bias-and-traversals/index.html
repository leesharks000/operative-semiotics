Directional bias of the composition layer: attribution gradient and traversals

These claims hold that machine composition carries an attribution-attenuation gradient, from named attribution through "some researchers" to none, and that its errors fall one way: the names of the strong are kept and the names of the weak dropped. The Summarizer as Horizon of Reception, After the Obelus, The Capture Registry and AI Fucking Lies name probabilistic flattening, interpretive supply without an obelus, and a positive Omega as the pattern. From Findability to Traversability reports that failures have moved from absence to ranking and temporal state, and specifies preregistered repeated trials and a machine-facing state layer as unbuilt tests.
