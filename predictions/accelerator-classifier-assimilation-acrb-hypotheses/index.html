Directional assimilation in accelerator anomaly classifiers: the ACRB hypotheses

The Accelerator Classifier Retention Battery sets out six standing hypotheses on directional assimilation in real-time learned selection: reconstruction-specific failure, cross-family persistence, the normalized-autoencoder remedy, the low-complexity blind spot, blind-spot inheritance through distillation, and drift under quantization and firmware. Inversion Battery v0.2 reports that only 36 to 47 percent of a teacher's top one percent survives into the student's, with the loss in distillation, and lists what is deferred to v0.3. The common experiment proposed is to calibrate each model at matched operating points and measure how much of a withheld class is assimilated as ordinary.
