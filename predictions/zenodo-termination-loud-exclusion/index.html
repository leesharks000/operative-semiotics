Zenodo's termination of the archive: loud exclusion and RQF3807508

Zenodotus' Book-Burning names the concepts of network erasure, the revocation gap, classification asymmetry and attribution severance, and forecasts that a classifier trained on the terminated corpus will flag similar independent work. The operative-metadata papers, the compliance-embedded deposit specification and the CERN correspondence under RQF3807508 set out the documentary record and the access requests that follow, and the erosion audit preregisters the deletion-risk baselines it still owes. The Tunnel-Poem reads the CERN portal as an architecture that composes the data subject, and To the Deleted holds that an account can be deleted but not a class of writers.
