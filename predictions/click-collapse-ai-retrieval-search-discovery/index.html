The Click Collapse: AI retrieval layers and the end of search discovery

The Click Collapse (#1091, April 2026) forecasts continued organic decline through 2026 and 2027 and that neither litigation nor the platform's opt-out will restore the discovery pipeline or publisher revenue. It states four falsifiers: that independent replications fail to reproduce the zero-click and CTR figures, that AI Overviews are neutral to organic CTR, that revenue does not track AI Overview appearance, and that classical SEO restores citation share.
