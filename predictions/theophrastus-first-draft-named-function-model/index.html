Theophrastus as the first draft of Aristotle: the named-function model

These claims read the Platonic, Theophrastan and Aristotelian names as functions of one method: dramatizing, registering, organizing. The First Draft (#1585, #1594, #1605), The Registry Has One Column (#1584) and Many and One (#1576) argue that the Characters are the dialogue's material before theory, that irony runs the whole line, and that succession is encoded as authorship; #1594 holds that the first axis of function-word variation is world/apparatus rather than author. The Measurement Register (#1570) and the notebook (#1569) argue Aristotle absorbed Platonic operations; Locating the Dependencies (#1602) corrects its own v2 on the Diogenes evidence. #1605 reports that its Aristophanes control separates real authors at 94% while the Aristotle/Theophrastus partition gives chance. A later review (#1658, October 2026) holds that 'first draft' must become testable and states the asymmetric predictions. The page also holds unrelated claims on the torus, Hegel's vocabulary, Hub work order and the arch-philosopher mantle.
