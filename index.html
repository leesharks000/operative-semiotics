The discipline’s research program: twenty-one open commitments, sorted by the kind of labour that resolves them. Registered predictions that resolve by observation on a date; experiments whose protocols are frozen and unrun; instruments not yet built. Every item states its blocker, its next action, and what a reader would need in hand — seven require nothing but a browser, curl, or python.
